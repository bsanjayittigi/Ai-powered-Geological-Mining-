1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CORPORATE HIGHLIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
During Fiscal Year 2024-25, Gevra Mega Opencast Project achieved an all-time record
Run-of-Mine (ROM) coal production of 52.40 Million Tonnes (MT), surpassing the annual
contractual target of 50.00 MT, which represents a performance achievement rate of 104.8%.
Raw coal despatches across all logistics routes aggregated to 51.90 MT, catering to pithead
super-thermal power stations (NTPC Korba, Sipat) and distant power utilities across Western India.

4. OVERBURDEN REMOVAL & HEMM FLEET AVAILABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Overburden Removal (OBR) for FY 2024-25 reached 194.40 Million Cubic Metres (M.Cum).
Fleet availability for 42 cum rope shovels was maintained at 84.6%, and 240-tonne dumpers
achieved 82.1% operational availability under computerized dispatch monitoring (DISPATCH®).
