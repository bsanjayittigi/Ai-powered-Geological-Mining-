1. GEOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK & REGIONAL STRATIGRAPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Jharia Coalfield represents a premier depository of prime and medium coking coal
in India, belonging to the Gondwana Supergroup (Permian Barakar Formation). Detailed
core drilling across Sector C identified continuous deposition of Seam XIV and Seam XIII
with minimal faulting and structural dips ranging between 4° and 7° south-westerly.

2. SEAM XIV THICKNESS & LITHOLOGICAL PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Seam XIV exhibits a persistent average gross thickness of 8.45 metres across exploratory
boreholes BH-12 through BH-28. The coal horizon comprises bright banded vitrain-rich
coking coal with occasional carbonaceous shale partings ranging from 0.15 to 0.40 metres.

3. PETROGRAPHIC & COKING QUALITY CHARACTERISTICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Proximate analysis conducted on 60 mesh washed core composites yielded:
- Mean Vitrinite Reflectance (Ro max): 1.18% - 1.24% (Prime Coking Band)
- Crucible Swelling Number (CSN): 6.5 to 7.0
- Volatile Matter (d.a.f.): 24.8% to 26.2%
- Coking Coal Classification: Steel Grade I (Ash <= 15.0% after beneficiation)

4. ESTIMATED RESERVES IN BLOCK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Proved Mineable Reserves in Seam XIV Sector C: 42.60 Million Tonnes.
Inferred Geological Reserves: 68.40 Million Tonnes.
